## 4. Required strategic expansion

### A. Portfolio architecture

Analyze the business as a portfolio of real, independently definable services.

Include:

- Unternehmensberatung and organizational consulting.
    
- Arbeits- und Lernpsychologie / Sequencing.
    
- Individual coaching and mentoring.
    
- Movement, martial arts and self-defense education.
    
- Dance, somatic methods and embodied practices.
    
- Meditation, mindfulness and workshops.
    
- Educational engagements with eligible institutions.
    
- Artistic performances and eligible creative work.
    
- Group programs, retreats and hybrid offers.
    
- Digital products, educational materials and product sales.
    

Identify which combinations can legitimately coexist, be sold separately or form an integrated package.

Evaluate the actual commercial substance of each model. Do not assume every combination can be separated for every legal purpose.

### B. Prospective optimization scenarios

Expand R10 beyond the single fixed-revenue baseline.

Compare meaningful combinations of:

- Revenue allocation across genuinely different services.
    
- Teaching versus consulting profit.
    
- B2B versus B2C engagements.
    
- Multiple clients versus concentrated client relationships.
    
- Eligible nonprofit/educational engagements.
    
- Artistic work where KSK conditions may apply.
    
- Different operating margins and deductible costs.
    
- Relevant turnover, income and contribution thresholds.
    
- 2026 and 2027 growth projections.
    

Analyze threshold crossings and marginal effects instead of only comparing annual totals.

Determine when a strategy is financially beneficial, neutral, disadvantageous or legally conditional.

Do not assume that statutory business-wide limits can be multiplied by changing labels or separating invoices.

### C. Offer and invoicing strategy

For promising models, explain:

- Which actual services would be delivered.
    
- How the offer could be packaged and priced.
    
- Which deliverables and activities support its classification.
    
- Which alternative truthful invoice descriptions are appropriate.
    
- Which records demonstrate the underlying facts.
    
- Which tax and insurance consequences follow.
    

Do not enforce one supposedly safest description for every engagement.

The operator may choose among genuinely provided services and may deliberately design future offerings around lawful distinctions.

Invoice language must still describe the relevant transaction accurately.

### D. Financial optimization

Compare:

- Total revenue and profit.
    
- Income tax and applicable trade tax.
    
- VAT consequences.
    
- DRV contribution obligations and benefit implications.
    
- TK/KSK and other insurance costs.
    
- Investment and expense deductions.
    
- Net available cash flow.
    
- Business effort, scalability and growth potential.
    
- Legal certainty and required administrative work.
    

Optimize the overall economic result, not merely one tax line or the lowest mandatory contribution.

Assess genuine 2026 expenditure decisions against 2027 alternatives.
