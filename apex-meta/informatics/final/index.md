## 2. Unified Research Bucket (`research/`)

All research conducted across all iterations is deterministically preserved here without altering original bytes:

```text
research/
├── 01-universal-instruction-research/   <- 1–2 week old foundational research (module-deepening/ A01..A13, C01..C03, handoffs/, patches/)
├── 02-deep-reasoning/                   <- DeepReasoning pass (00-master-matrix through 07-candidate-always-on-block, modules/)
├── 03-research-of-file-10/              <- Yesterday's 10-packet research results (RESULTS-01 through RESULTS-11-synthesis)
├── 04-finalization-dossier/             <- Step-by-step audit dossier (00-research-critique through 08-complete-comparison)
├── 05-analysis-after-research/          <- Post-research analysis notes
└── 06-layered-rule-kb/                  <- Layered Rule Knowledge Base (all 22 rules, 5 module audits, index.md)
```
