- **Before:** `<align when="the task is not a single trivial edit or a direct question">` in `specs/apex_contract_v1.0_ultra.xml`; `EM-13` in `specs/execution-modes/task-specification.md` said "a task is anything other than a single trivial edit or a direct question".

### C2. Intensity lever `EM-06a`
- **Before:** `specs/execution-modes/qa-decision-matrix.md` had `EM-06` (matrix or skip) and sections 2.1-2.2 only. No rule scaled depth with the stakes.

- **Solution:** section 2.3 adds `EM-06a [P1 | I85/E40/R30]`, levels `L0`-`L3`, keyed on the question's own `I/E/R`; low `E` raises the level; thresholds are illustrative and unmeasured. Vendored to Leela in `ba84c510`.
