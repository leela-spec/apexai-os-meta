+# Upload retry limit raised to five
+
+## Change
+
+The maximum number of retries for a failed upload increased from three to five.
+
+## Rationale
+
+Upload failures are often transient. Some uploads recover on the fourth or fifth retry, so the previous three-retry limit stopped recoverable uploads too early. Five retries improve completion while preserving a hard upper bound that prevents indefinite retries and uncontrolled request load.
+
+This decision changes only the retry limit. Existing retry eligibility and backoff rules remain unchanged.
