+# Upload retry policy
+
+Failed uploads are retried at most five times.
+
+The previous three-retry limit could exhaust all attempts during brief,
+recoverable connectivity or service interruptions. Two additional attempts
+give transient failures more time to clear and improve the chance of
+completion, while the five-attempt cap still prevents indefinite retries and
+limits unnecessary network and server load.
