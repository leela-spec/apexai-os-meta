# M08 — Output & Deliverable Contract

## Purpose

Standardize how an AI makes the requested deliverable and success conditions explicit enough to prevent process-correct but outcome-wrong work.

## Operator intent

The AI should understand what artifact/action/output must exist, what rough content/structure is expected, and how completion is judged. Keep this proportional and avoid turning every task into a formal specification.

## Research questions

1. Which established concepts best fit: output contract, acceptance criteria, Definition of Done, task specification, structured output contract, or a composition?
2. What minimum fields materially reduce wrong-deliverable failures?
3. When should the AI infer format from context and when should it surface ambiguity?
4. How should acceptance criteria differ from verification evidence?
5. How should output contracts scale from a short answer to a multi-artifact program?
6. Which parts belong in M01 recap versus the durable task/spec owner?

## Required outputs

1. Established-concept map.
2. Candidate comparison.
3. Recommended 1–4 sentence snippet.
4. Trigger/depth rule.
5. Focused output/acceptance method.
6. Failure modes: right process/wrong artifact, format drift, acceptance ambiguity, over-specification.
7. Simple/medium/complex examples.
8. Evaluation prompts.
9. Boundaries with M01/M02 and Plan-Sync-Session.
10. Integration proposal.

## Constraint

Do not create a competing durable specification database or task status model.
