# Packaging Ideas

Wrapper material: compostable cellulose film, or paper with a thin wax-free barrier. Foil is out because it is not recyclable in Germany.

Resealing: a folded paper sleeve with a tuck flap that lets the three pieces stay protected after the first piece is eaten. A sticker seal is the fallback.

Idea: print a QR code inside the sleeve, not on the outside, so it appears after opening. It links to the joint landing page with the ritual audio.

Open question: does a paper barrier stop cocoa butter from migrating to the surface over eight weeks?

Alex likes the sleeve idea best. Chris prefers a simple wrapper for the first test.
