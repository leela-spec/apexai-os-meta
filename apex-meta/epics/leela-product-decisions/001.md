# Reconcile stale decision-ledger state against authoritative decision records

## Acceptance Criteria

- Compare registry/open-question rows against current SSOT-D decision records.

- Reconciliation report separates already-answered/stale rows from truly open rows.
- Proposed ledger repairs are explicit and source-backed.
