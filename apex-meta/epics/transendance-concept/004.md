# Define facilitation and safety boundaries for the concept draft

## Acceptance Criteria

- Represent consent/opt-out and intensity modulation at concept level.
- Emotional-release language does not imply forced catharsis.
- Distinguish psychological framing from clinical therapy claims.
- Represent grounding/closure requirements.

## Definition of Done

- Concept contains explicit non-clinical facilitation and participant-agency boundaries.
