# Automate decision-feedback collection and review

## Acceptance Criteria

- New operator decisions can enter feedback record with low friction.
- Scheduled/event-based review can retrieve original thesis/evidence.
- Current outcome data can be attached from approved sources.
- Feedback compares expectation, evidence, and outcome without rewriting original decision.
- Automation produces review/learning output but does not place trades.

## Definition of Done

- At least one real decision can travel through capture to later automated feedback.
