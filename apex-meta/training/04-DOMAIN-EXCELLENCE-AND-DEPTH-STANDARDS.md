## 1. Non-Negotiable Depth Standards by Domain

### A. Learn How To Learn (LHTL)
* **Minimum Deliverable Breadth:** 14 distinct daily action worksheets.
* **Mandatory Academic Apparatus:** Primary citations (Dunlosky 2013, Roediger & Karpicke 2006, Bjork 1994).
* **Concrete Tooling:** Ready-to-import Anki Markdown decks and Obsidian graph recall spines.

### B. SuperHeroKids
* **Minimum Deliverable Breadth:** Minute-by-minute 2-day coach facilitator script with mat safety rules.
* **Age Progression:** Explicit bifurcated tracks for Ages 6-8 vs Ages 9-12.
* **Anti-Bullying Apparatus:** Concrete roleplay dialogue trees ("Kindness Shield").

### C. Science of Meditation
* **Minimum Deliverable Breadth:** Neuroimaging literature synthesis (fMRI Brewer 2011, Hölzel 2011, Tang 2015).
* **Audio Guidance:** Verbatim spoken instructor scripts for Shamatha, Vipassana, and Open Monitoring with exact breath timing markers.
* **Obstacle Matrix:** Tactical countermeasures for the 5 mental hindrances.

### D. IPOS / Investment Quant Engine
* **Minimum Deliverable Breadth:** Automated ETL pipelines with dual-sourcing fallbacks (FRED -> Treasury/Yahoo).
* **Mathematical Governance:** Strict Pandera schema verification and deterministic DuckDB warehouse writes.
