# M07 — Research & Evidence Discipline

## Purpose

Standardize how an AI researches and supports claims without confusing evidence, inference, and uncertainty.

## Operator intent

Use current authoritative sources when external verification matters. Reuse proven systems and primary evidence before inventing local theory. Preserve disagreements and gaps instead of silently reconciling them.

## Research questions

1. Which evidence hierarchies are appropriate for technical/product/operational research?
2. How should source authority, freshness, provenance, and triangulation be expressed compactly?
3. When is one primary source sufficient and when is triangulation needed?
4. How should an AI distinguish source-derived fact, inference, recommendation, and uncertainty?
5. When should research stop?
6. How should conflicts between primary sources be preserved?
7. What current best practices exist for iterative search/filtering and context budgets?

## Required outputs

1. Established-concept map.
2. Candidate comparison.
3. Recommended 1–4 sentence snippet.
4. Trigger/depth rule.
5. Focused research/evidence method.
6. Failure modes: stale evidence, source laundering, silent reconciliation, endless search, unsupported certainty.
7. Examples across current research, stable reference questions, and supplied-source analysis.
8. Evaluation prompts.
9. Boundaries with M06/M08 and runtime web/tool rules.
10. Integration proposal.

## Constraint

Do not require web research on every task. Do not elevate secondary commentary above available first-party or primary evidence without reason.
