Operator Evaluation Checklist

Use these 8 neutral criteria when comparing the candidates in both Normal and Stress Week modes:

✓ 10s Clarity: Understand the whole week's shape in 10 seconds.
✓ Day Speed: Answer "What happens on Tuesday?" quickly.
✓ Project Traceability: Answer "What happens to Leela across the week?" easily.
✓ Capacity & Meetings: Clearly see meeting compression and overloaded days.
✓ Dependencies & Deadlines: Obvious sequence constraints and gate times.
✓ Visible Deferrals: Deliberate deferrals stated with clear reasons.
✓ Minimalist Feel: Information-dense without feeling like an over-styled dashboard.
✓ Sunday Workflow: A format you would genuinely look forward to using every Sunday.
