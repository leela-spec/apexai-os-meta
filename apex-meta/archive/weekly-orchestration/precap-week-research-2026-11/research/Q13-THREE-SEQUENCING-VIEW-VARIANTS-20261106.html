Variation A — Weekly Calendar Grid

Mon–Sat overview with sequence cards inside actual day columns. Best for cross-day scheduling and spotting overloaded days. Horizontal scroll preserves readable sprint content on smaller screens.

Variation B — Day Cards

Each day is a readable vertical set of sequence blocks, with sprint topics directly below them. Best for daily clarity and mobile reading; harder to compare exact free windows across six days at once.

Variation C — Sequence Board + Inspector

Sequence rows show how block composition changes across the week. Select a block to inspect source work references, sprint topics and mixed-project allocation. Best for coordinated planning/review, but more complex than a static document.
