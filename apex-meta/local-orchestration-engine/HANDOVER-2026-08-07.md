## What FEE is, in one paragraph

**Flow Execution Engine (FEE)** is a stdlib-only Python CLI that replaces the *human actor* at
**step 4 ("operator execution")** of the already-live Weekly Orchestrator loop — nothing more. It
is explicitly **not** a third orchestration system (APEX OS has exactly two: Weekly Orchestrator
and Multi-Agent Orchestration — see `.claude/CLAUDE.md` at the repo root). It reads an existing
flow packet + prompt pack, drives the actual reasoning out to browser-based AI subscription
sessions (Claude sanctioned channel first) or hands work to the operator as a worklist, and writes
output in the same "raw notes" shape step 5 (`apex-evidence-normalize`) already expects. Gate G3
stays a required human checkpoint throughout — FEE changes who *performs* step 4, never who
*approves* it.
