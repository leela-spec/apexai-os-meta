# David Anderson

Neurobiologist at the California Institute of Technology (Caltech).

## Research & Collaboration

- **Book**: Co-authored *The Neuroscience of Emotion* with [[entities/ralph-adolphs|Ralph Adolphs]].
- **Circuit Analysis**: Investigates hypothalamic circuits, aggression, and neuropeptide mechanisms responsible for temporal persistence and scalability of emotional states in model organisms (mice and Drosophila).
