# Dr. Ralph Adolphs

Professor of Psychology, Neuroscience, and Biology at the California Institute of Technology (Caltech).

## Academic Background & Contributions

- **Functional Emotion Architecture**: Co-authored *The Neuroscience of Emotion* with [[entities/david-anderson|David Anderson]], advocating for the [[concepts/functional-theory-of-emotion|Functional Theory of Emotion]] across species.
- **Lesion & Neuropsychology Research**: Led groundbreaking studies with [[entities/patient-sm|Patient SM]] and other focal lesion patients at Iowa and Caltech, establishing the neural basis of facial emotion recognition and fear processing.
- **Editorial & Scientific Leadership**: Former co-editor of the journal *Affective Science*.
- **Personal Practice**: Applies autonomic regulation (cold exposure / ice baths) and cognitive transitions (5-minute silence before lab meetings), while training for a 100-mile ultramarathon following cancer recovery.
