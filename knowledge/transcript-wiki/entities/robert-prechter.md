# Robert Prechter

Author, market analyst, and founder of [[entities/elliott-wave-international|Elliott Wave International]].

## Contributions

- Discovered and recovered [[entities/ralph-nelson-elliott|R.N. Elliott]]'s writings from obscure microfilm archives in the 1970s.
- Published foundational texts that popularized [[concepts/elliott-wave-principle|Elliott Wave Principle]] during the 1980s.
- Made a famous counter-consensus prediction in the late 1970s/early 1980s forecasting a "repeat of the Roaring Twenties" bull market during 18% interest rates and high inflation.
