# Abdullah

**Abdullah** was an esoteric mystic and Ethiopian rabbi living in New York City during the 1930s who mentored [[entities/neville-goddard|Neville Goddard]] in Hebrew, Kabbalistic symbolism, and the mechanics of mental assumption.

## Core Legacy & Demonstrations

- **The Barbados Demonstration**: When a penniless Neville desired to travel from New York to Barbados to visit family, Abdullah famously refused to discuss logistical travel details, declaring: *"You are in Barbados, and you went first class."* Neville was instructed to sleep in his Manhattan bed while mentally and sensorially experiencing sleeping in his family home in Barbados. Within weeks, an unexpected gift of funds and a first-class steamship ticket materialized without conscious solicitation.
- **Uncompromising Mental Posture**: Taught that the human mind cannot debate or question an assumption once made; opening the door to discussion or doubt destroys the imaginal state.
- **Quieting the Physical Body**: Emphasized to Neville that intellectual imagining is ineffective if the physical body remains tense and argumentative; the sensory apparatus must be quieted before assumption can imprint upon the subconscious.
