# Ralph Nelson Elliott

Accountant (1871–1948) and creator of the [[concepts/elliott-wave-principle|Elliott Wave Principle]].

## Key Discoveries & History

- While bedridden due to severe illness in the 1930s, spent years examining market price and time series.
- Discovered recurring fractal price patterns across multiple timeframes.
- Successfully called the bottom of the Great Crash of 1929 and advised market participants to buy at depression lows.
