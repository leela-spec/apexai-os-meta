# Neville Goddard

**Neville Lancelot Goddard** (1905–1972) was an influential esoteric author and lecturer in the New Thought movement who pioneered the [[concepts/law-of-assumption|Law of Assumption]].

## Core Teachings & Philosophy

- **Consciousness as the Only Reality**: Taught that the external physical world is an outpicturing of human consciousness ("the world is yourself pushed out").
- **Imagination as Divine Creative Power**: Defined imagination not as fantasy, but as the actual spiritual substance that structures human experience.
- **Practical Somatic Methods**: Developed accessible techniques for shifting internal states, notably the [[concepts/falling-backward-technique|Falling Backward Technique]], [[concepts/state-akin-to-sleep|State Akin to Sleep]], and [[concepts/morning-revision-protocol|Morning Revision Protocol]].
- **Lineage**: Studied in New York City during the 1930s under the Ethiopian rabbi and mystic [[entities/abdullah|Abdullah]], learning Hebrew, esoteric scripture interpretation, and radical mental discipline.
