# Elliott Wave International

Independent technical analysis and market forecasting firm.

## Key Offerings & History

- Founded by [[entities/robert-prechter|Robert Prechter]] to publish market research based on the [[concepts/elliott-wave-principle|Elliott Wave Principle]].
- Operates educational resources including **Waveopedia** (encyclopedia of wave patterns) and **Trader's Classroom** (educational training for self-directed practitioners).
- Successfully anticipated major historical trend shifts including interest rate cycles transitioning from zero/negative bounds to multi-decade highs.
