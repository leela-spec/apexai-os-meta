# Bank of America Global Research

Institutional investment research unit of Bank of America.

## Key Research Products

- **Global Fund Manager Survey (FMS)**: Monthly survey of institutional asset allocators.
- **Contrarian Sentiment Rules**: Uses cash thresholds (<4.5% or <3.5%) and crowded trade rankings to generate contrarian sell and buy signals.
