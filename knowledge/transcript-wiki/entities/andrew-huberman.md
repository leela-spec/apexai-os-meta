# Andrew Huberman

Professor of Neurobiology and Ophthalmology at Stanford School of Medicine, scientific educator, and author.

## Public Science & Communication

- **Podcast**: Host of the *Huberman Lab Podcast*, focusing on science-based tools for health and human performance.
- **Author**: Authored *Protocols: An Operating Manual for the Human Body* (hosted 2026 launch events in NYC Radio City Music Hall on Sept 17, LA Dolby Theater on Oct 8, and SF Masonic on Oct 28).
- **Research Interests**: Neural plasticity, vision, stress response, and circadian biology.
