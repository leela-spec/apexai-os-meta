# Patient SM

A landmark neuropsychological case study in human affective neuroscience.

## Clinical History & Experimental Findings

- **Pathology**: Suffers from Urbach-Wiethe disease, causing selective bilateral calcification and destruction of the amygdala.
- **Exteroceptive Fear Deficit**: Displays complete absence of fear or avoidance to external threats (spiders, venomous snakes, haunted houses).
- **Interoceptive Panic Preservation**: When exposed to 35% carbon dioxide inhalation, Patient SM experienced acute fear and a full-blown panic attack, proving that suffocation panic is mediated by brainstem interoceptive circuits independent of the amygdala.
