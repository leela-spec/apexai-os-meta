# Elliott Prechter

Computer scientist and financial software engineer specializing in quantitative [[concepts/elliott-wave-principle|Elliott Wave]] automation.

## Background & Contributions

- **Background**: Educated in computer science and engineering; became acutely engaged with financial market cycles during the 2008 financial crisis (shorting banking equities 4x).
- **Software Development**: Modernized and redesigned [[concepts/e-waves|E-Waves]], an algorithmic engine originally conceived in 1986 to automate objective wave counting and partial pattern extrapolation.
- **Analytical Stance**: Advocates using Elliott Wave primarily as an objective risk-management tool to define falsification/invalidation levels rather than purely dogmatic long-term forecasting.
