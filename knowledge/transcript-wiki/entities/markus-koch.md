# Markus Koch

German financial journalist and long-time Wall Street correspondent.

## Operations & Media Platform

- **Platforms**: Founder of `markuskoch.de` (rebranded and expanded from `360wallstreet.de`) delivering equity research and investment education.
- **Expansion**: Established a dedicated broadcast and production studio in Munich opening December.
- **Focus**: Provides daily pre-market analysis ("Opening Bell") and post-market reviews ("Closing Bell"), analyzing macroeconomic data, earnings calls, and sentiment indicators.
