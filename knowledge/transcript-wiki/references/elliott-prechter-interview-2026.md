# Elliott Prechter Interview on Elliott Wave and E-Waves (2026)

Interview exploring the evolution of [[concepts/elliott-wave-principle|Elliott Wave Theory]], its automation via [[concepts/e-waves|E-Waves]], and current financial market dynamics.

## Key Discussion Points

- **Historical Backdrop**: [[entities/ralph-nelson-elliott|Ralph Nelson Elliott]] developed the principle while bedridden in the early 1930s, successfully calling the end of the 1929 Great Crash. [[entities/robert-prechter|Robert Prechter]] recovered the theory from microfilm in the 1970s and popularized it in the 1980s by forecasting the repeat of the Roaring Twenties during 18% interest rate stagflation.
- **Stationary Model**: Market psychological patterns have remained invariant for over 200 years despite technological shifts, algorithmic trading, and quantitative models ([[concepts/market-stationarity|Market Stationarity]]).
- **E-Waves Automation**: Originally started in 1986, modern computing power enables automated pattern extrapolation, solving partial pattern ambiguity without hardcoded subjective guidelines.
- **Current Market Regime**: Observations on [[concepts/fifth-wave-characteristics|Fifth Wave Dynamics]]—thin breadth, speculative mania (NFTs), and the [[concepts/nasdaq-bitcoin-divergence|NASDAQ-Bitcoin divergence]] as a modern Dow Theory warning signal.
- **Deceptive Patterns**: The [[concepts/flat-pattern|Flat Pattern]] identified as the most dangerous corrective pattern due to trap breakouts to nominal new highs.
