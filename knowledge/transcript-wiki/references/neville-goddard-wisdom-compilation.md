# 3 Hours of Neville Goddard Wisdom Compilation

A comprehensive audio lecture collection presenting the core teachings and esoteric methods of [[entities/neville-goddard|Neville Goddard]], detailing the practical application of the [[concepts/law-of-assumption|Law of Assumption]].

## Core Architectural & Methodological Insights

1. **[[concepts/falling-backward-technique|The Falling Backward Technique]] (Early Region)**:
   A somatic method utilizing the physical sensation of falling backward into a soft, infinite cushion at bedtime to quiet sensory tyranny and bypass ego resistance, allowing the feeling of the wish fulfilled to imprint directly upon the subconscious.

2. **[[concepts/state-akin-to-sleep|State Akin to Sleep (SATS)]] (Early/Middle Region)**:
   The hypnagogic twilight state between waking and sleeping where the physical senses are muted and imaginal acts are experienced with sensory vividness and naturalness without muscular exertion.

3. **[[concepts/morning-revision-protocol|Morning Revision Protocol]] (Middle Region)**:
   The disciplined practice of spending exactly 20 minutes each morning (ideally between 6:00 AM and 8:00 AM) mentally revising discordant events from the previous day into their ideal outcomes until the imaginal memory feels more natural than the sensory fact.

4. **[[concepts/inner-conversations|Inner Conversations & Mental Diet]] (Middle Region)**:
   The continuous internal dialogue that shapes waking consciousness. Goddard emphasizes that "there is no such thing as casual imagination" and daytime mental arguments will neutralize nighttime imaginal acts unless brought into alignment.

5. **[[concepts/feeling-as-causal-state|Feeling as Causal State]] (Middle/Late Region)**:
   The epistemological inversion asserting that feeling is a causal creative state rather than an emotional reaction to external physical circumstances (e.g. one must feel wealthy in consciousness before physical money appears).

6. **[[concepts/bridge-of-incidents|The Bridge of Incidents]] (Late Region)**:
   The natural, unpredictable sequence of physical events, encounters, and opportunities through which an assumption externalizes into the world without conscious micro-management.

7. **[[concepts/sabbath-of-assumption|The Sabbath of Assumption]] (Late Region)**:
   The psychological state of complete internal rest and non-striving following an imaginal act, characterized by the refusal to check for physical evidence, test the law, or doubt the outcome.

## Epistemic Context
- **Attribution**: Teachings represent the metaphysical and phenomenological philosophy of [[entities/neville-goddard|Neville Goddard]] and his teacher [[entities/abdullah|Abdullah]], presented as spiritual law rather than empirical natural science.
