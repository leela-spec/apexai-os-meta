# NASDAQ-Bitcoin Divergence

An intermarket analytical concept comparing tech equities and cryptocurrency momentum.

## Analytical Dynamics

- **Risk-On Classification**: Contrary to "digital gold" safe-haven claims, empirical price action shows Bitcoin behaving as a high-beta risk-on asset tightly correlated with the NASDAQ 100.
- **Divergence as Leading Indicator**: When NASDAQ makes new highs while Bitcoin fails to participate, this divergence acts as a modern analog to Dow Theory non-confirmations ("Dow Theory 2.0").
- **Fifth-Wave Context**: Non-confirmation between leading risk barometers frequently marks late-stage [[concepts/fifth-wave-characteristics|Fifth Wave Dynamics]].
