# Emotion Operating Features

Four foundational operational dimensions established by [[entities/ralph-adolphs|Ralph Adolphs]] and [[entities/david-anderson|David Anderson]] that distinguish emotional states from reflexes and simple cognitions:

## The Four Pillars

1. **Priority (Preemption)**:
   - Emotions serve as interrupt mechanisms (Herbert Simon) that override ongoing goal-directed behavior to address critical survival threats.
2. **Valence**:
   - The fundamental approach-avoidance / pleasant-unpleasant axis (Darwin's principle of antithesis).
3. **Scalability (Graded Intensity)**:
   - Unlike binary all-or-nothing reflexes, emotional states scale continuously according to threat imminence (e.g., anxiety → distal fear → proximal panic).
4. **Temporal Persistence**:
   - Emotional motivational states endure over time after the initial triggering stimulus ceases, mediated neurochemically by slow neuropeptide release. Demonstrated conclusively in [[concepts/temporal-persistence-in-amnesia|Iowa amnesia studies]].
