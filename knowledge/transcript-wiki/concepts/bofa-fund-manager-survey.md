# Bank of America Fund Manager Survey

A monthly institutional sentiment gauge conducted by [[entities/bank-of-america-global-research|Bank of America Global Research]].

## Core Metrics & Thresholds

- **Cash Levels**: Average portfolio cash held by global asset managers. Normal contrarian sell signals trigger when cash falls below 4.5%. August survey registered an extreme reading below 3.5% (down from 3.6% in July), marking the 3rd most bullish reading since 2022.
- **Equity Allocation**: Global equity allocations reached highest levels since November 2021.
- **Crowded Trade Tracking**: Identifies single-direction positioning extremes; Long Global Semiconductors ranked #1 most crowded trade (53% of respondents, down from 82% in July).
- **Rate Expectations**: 72% of respondents projected no Federal Reserve interest rate hikes prior to November midterms.
