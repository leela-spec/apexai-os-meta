# E-Waves

A quantitative software engine designed to perform automated, objective [[concepts/elliott-wave-principle|Elliott Wave]] analysis.

## Development & Mechanism

- **History**: Initial V1 created in 1986; sat dormant due to extreme compute limitations until recent hardware advancements enabled full algorithmic modernization by [[entities/elliott-prechter|Elliott Prechter]].
- **Elimination of Subjectivity**: Removes human cognitive biases and rigid human guidelines by learning pattern behavior automatically while adhering strictly to foundational structural wave rules.
- **Handling Incomplete Data**: Real-time market charts consist largely of partial patterns. E-Waves performs dynamic extrapolation—simulating and completing candidate structures in memory to evaluate probability distributions.
- **Cross-Market Universality**: Uses unified model weights across asset classes (equities, commodities, bonds, crypto) to prevent overfitting, while recognizing asset-specific structural tendencies (e.g. commodity 5th wave blow-offs).
