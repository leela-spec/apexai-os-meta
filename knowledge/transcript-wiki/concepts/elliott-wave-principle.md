# Elliott Wave Principle

A theory of financial market price structure based on repetitive wave patterns in price and time.

## Core Ideas

- **Pattern Genesis**: Developed by [[entities/ralph-nelson-elliott|Ralph Nelson Elliott]] in the 1930s; identifies 5-wave impulse phases (1-2-3-4-5) followed by 3-wave corrective phases (A-B-C).
- **Core Value**: Acts as a disciplined risk management tool by identifying exact price/time levels where a market thesis is objectively falsified.
- **Rule Baseline vs. Guidelines**: Fixed rules strictly constrain legal wave counts, while subjective guidelines and probabilistic alternative counts explain differences between human analysts.
- **Psychological Invariance**: Because human emotional extremes (fear, greed, herd behavior) are constant, the wave structure remains invariant over centuries ([[concepts/market-stationarity|Market Stationarity]]).
