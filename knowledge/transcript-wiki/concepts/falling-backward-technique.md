# Falling Backward Technique

A specialized somatic method taught by [[entities/neville-goddard|Neville Goddard]] designed to overcome sensory tyranny and intellectual ego resistance when entering the [[concepts/state-akin-to-sleep|State Akin to Sleep (SATS)]].

## Protocol & Mechanics

1. **Preparation**: Lie down comfortably in bed at night, preparing for sleep. Select a single, clear desired state in advance (avoiding assuming multiple desires simultaneously).
2. **Somatic Induction**: Rather than straining to visualize complex visual scenes, deliberately induce the physical sensation of falling backward — as though the bed is gently tilting and the body is descending into a soft, infinite cushion.
3. **Surrender of the Ego**: As the physical sensation of falling occurs, the body experiences a momentary reflex of surrender ("a little death" of the conscious sensory grip).
4. **Assumption Imprinting**: At the exact threshold of surrender and drowsiness, assume the feeling of the wish fulfilled. The assumption enters unopposed because sensory debate is silenced.
5. **Release into Sleep**: Crucially, do not attempt to maintain conscious mental effort. Release the assumption entirely into the subconscious as sleep takes over.

## Common Pitfalls & Warnings
- **Visual Straining**: Attempting to force visual imagery while falling creates mental tension; the method relies on physical sensation and felt emotional tone, not mental pictures.
- **Over-Controlling**: Trying to consciously monitor the transition to sleep prevents the natural subconscious integration.
