# Fifth Wave Characteristics

Distinctive dynamics of the final advance in a 5-wave impulse sequence within the [[concepts/elliott-wave-principle|Elliott Wave Principle]].

## Distinctive Signatures

- **Breadth Divergence**: Wave 3 exhibits broad market participation ("thick breadth" where the vast majority of equities participate). In contrast, Wave 5 exhibits extreme narrowing ("thin breadth"), where major cap-weighted indices rise on the backs of a few mega-cap names while median stocks decline.
- **Speculative Excess & Crowding**: Characterized by retail manias and extreme institutional crowding (e.g. 53% of fund managers long global semiconductors in the [[concepts/bofa-fund-manager-survey|BofA FMS]]).
- **Cash Depletion**: Extreme institutional optimism where global fund manager cash levels plunge below 3.5%, triggering historical contrarian sell signals.
- **Extension Potential**: Although generally shorter than Wave 3, fifth waves can experience substantial blow-off extensions, especially in commodity markets.
- **Terminal Structure**: Regardless of magnitude, Wave 5 constitutes the final sub-wave before a major trend reversal or multi-degree correction.
