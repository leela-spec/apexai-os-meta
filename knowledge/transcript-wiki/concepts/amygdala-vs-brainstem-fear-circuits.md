# Amygdala vs. Brainstem Fear Circuits

A fundamental dissociation in affective neurobiology demonstrating that "fear" is not a unitary monolithic entity in the human brain.

## The Double Dissociation

1. **Exteroceptive Threat Fear (Amygdala-Dependent)**:
   - Evaluates external sensory threats (predators, snakes, spiders, environmental hazards).
   - Abolished in [[entities/patient-sm|Patient SM]] and other patients with selective bilateral amygdala calcification.
2. **Interoceptive Suffocation Panic (Brainstem-Dependent)**:
   - Triggered by internal physiological distress, specifically blood acidosis / CO2 elevation causing severe air hunger.
   - Intact and fully inducible in amygdala-damaged patients via 35% CO2 inhalation (Feinstein, Adolphs, Hurlemann study).
   - Mediated by primitive brainstem and chemosensory reflex centers rather than the amygdala.
