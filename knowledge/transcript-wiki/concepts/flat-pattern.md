# Flat Pattern

A 3-3-5 corrective structure within the [[concepts/elliott-wave-principle|Elliott Wave Principle]].

## Structural Behavior & Psychological Trap

- **Bull/Bear Trap**: After an initial corrective decline (Wave A), Wave B rallies sharply back up to or slightly above the origin of Wave A, mimicking a strong trend continuation breakout.
- **Subsequent Crash**: Once market participants buy into the breakout, Wave C initiates a rapid, steep downward impulse that catches participants off-guard.
- **Significance**: Identified by [[entities/elliott-prechter|Elliott Prechter]] as the pattern causing the most severe market damage and behavioral confusion.
