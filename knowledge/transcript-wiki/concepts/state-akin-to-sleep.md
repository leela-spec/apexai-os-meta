# State Akin to Sleep (SATS)

The hypnagogic transitional state of consciousness between active waking awareness and deep sleep, central to [[entities/neville-goddard|Neville Goddard]]'s methodology.

## Operating Characteristics

- **Sensory Muting**: Voluntary motor activity and sensory awareness are quieted without losing conscious intentionality.
- **Effortless Imagination**: In this state, an imagined action or scene takes on sensory vividness and the psychological tone of reality without physical strain.
- **Direct Subconscious Access**: The critical analytical intellect (the conscious ego) ceases its habitual cross-examination, allowing imaginal assumptions to impress directly upon the subconscious memory.
- **Methods of Entry**: Can be entered through progressive relaxation, breath settling, or the somatic [[concepts/falling-backward-technique|Falling Backward Technique]].
