# Functional Theory of Emotion

The conceptual framework developed by [[entities/ralph-adolphs|Ralph Adolphs]] and [[entities/david-anderson|David Anderson]] defining emotions as evolved internal functional states.

## Architecture

- **Intermediate Control Level**: Situated between rigid, inflexible reflexes (e.g. patellar tendon tap, withdrawing from a hot stove) and unconstrained deliberate cognitive planning/volition.
- **Environmental Adaptation**: Evolved to coordinate multi-system physiological and behavioral responses to recurring ecological challenges (e.g. predators, conspecific competition, contamination).
- **Substrate Neutrality**: Can be implemented across human neural circuits, non-human animals (mice, flies, octopuses), and artificial intelligence architectures.
