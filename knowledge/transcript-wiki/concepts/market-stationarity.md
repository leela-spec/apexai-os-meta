# Market Stationarity

The empirical observation that financial market cycle structures do not structurally degrade or drift over centuries.

## Core Tenets

- **Human Primacy**: Even in markets dominated by high-frequency execution and algorithmic models, human engineers configure, supervise, and terminate those systems.
- **Invariance**: Wave mechanics, proportion, and crowd psychology observed in the 1800s and 1930s by [[entities/ralph-nelson-elliott|R.N. Elliott]] remain identical in modern automated equity and crypto markets.
- **Robustness**: Stationary statistical behavior justifies utilizing unified algorithmic models across diverse market environments without overfitting to recent regimes.
