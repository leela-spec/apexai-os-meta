# Law of Assumption

The foundational metaphysical philosophy taught by [[entities/neville-goddard|Neville Goddard]], asserting that subjective consciousness is the primary creative force of external reality.

## Core Principles

1. **"The World is Yourself Pushed Out"**:
   The physical world contains no independent causal power; external events, conditions, and people reflect the subconscious assumptions and self-concept of the observer.

2. **Persistence in Assumption**:
   *"An assumption though false, if persisted in, will harden into fact."* An individual does not attract what they merely wish for or desire, but what they assume themselves to already be in consciousness.

3. **Assumption vs. Pretense**:
   Goddard emphasizes a sharp distinction between intellectual pretense (repeating affirmations without internal conviction) and true assumption (an internal shift in feeling and identity where the new state feels natural and real).

4. **Non-Interference with Means**:
   The conscious ego is not responsible for engineering how an assumption materializes; the manifestation unfolds deterministically through the [[concepts/bridge-of-incidents|Bridge of Incidents]].
