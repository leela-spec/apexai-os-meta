# Insula and Interoception

The neuroanatomical hub responsible for mapping internal visceral and physiological states.

## Neuroanatomical Function

- **Visceral Convergence**: Receives ascending neural projections from all major internal organs (heart, gastrointestinal tract, kidneys, vascular system).
- **Interoceptive Awareness**: Acts as the cortical substrate translating bodily physiological perturbation (e.g. kidney stone visceral pain or autonomic arousal) into subjective feeling states (Bud Craig, Antonio Damasio somatic marker hypothesis).
- **Conceptual vs. Real Body Maps**: Clarifies that popular body map diagrams (Lauri Nummenmaa) reflect cognitive conceptual associations of emotion words rather than direct somatic telemetry.
