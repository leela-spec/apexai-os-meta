# Retail Earnings Expectations Bar

The phenomenon wherein lowered analyst expectations enable positive stock price reactions despite mixed underlying metrics.

## Mechanics & Case Studies

- **Expectation Reset**: Weak retail sales, softening consumer confidence, and labor market cooling lower the consensus bar prior to earnings releases.
- **Home Depot Example**: Delivered strong Q2 earnings and beat expectations aided by Trump-era tariff refunds; only maintained full-year guidance (effectively downgrading 2H implied growth), yet shares rallied.
- **Amer Sports vs. On Holding**: Amer Sports gained on 30% revenue growth and margin expansion despite cutting Q3 EPS guidance (32c vs 38c expected); in contrast, On Holding faced margin compression.
- **Nike Divergence**: Closed at 14-year lows (-78% off all-time highs, losing $220B in market cap), reflecting structural company-specific impairment.
