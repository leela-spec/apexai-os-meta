# Sabbath of Assumption

The psychological and spiritual state of complete internal rest following the successful implantation of an assumption, described by [[entities/neville-goddard|Neville Goddard]] as entering the "Sabbath."

## Core Principles

- **Psychological Non-Striving**: Once an assumption is established in the subconscious, mental labor and anxiety cease. The practitioner rests in the conviction that the seed has been planted and will grow naturally beneath the surface.
- **"Do Not Test the Law"**: Goddard repeatedly cautions practitioners against waking up the next morning and checking bank accounts or looking for physical signs to see "if it worked." Checking for results reveals that one has fallen out of the state of fulfillment back into the state of lack.
- **Sabbath as Inner Certainty**: The Sabbath is not physical inactivity, but internal certainty that what has been claimed in consciousness is already an established fact in reality.
