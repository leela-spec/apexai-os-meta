# Emotion vs. Feeling Dissociation

A crucial methodological distinction in affective neuroscience separating functional emotional states from subjective conscious experience.

## Epistemic Principles

- **The Consciousness Trap**: Conflating emotions with subjective conscious "feelings" (as in classic Jamesian or LeDoux models) halts comparative neuroscience because conscious experience cannot be directly empirically verified in non-human animals.
- **Scientific Parallel with Vision & Memory**: Just as vision researchers study photoreception and visual cortex processing without solving conscious visual qualia, affective scientists can study neural emotion circuits independently of consciousness.
- **AI & Cross-Species Applicability**: Enables valid scientific inquiry into emotional states in animal models (flies, rodents) and artificial agents without requiring subjective consciousness.
