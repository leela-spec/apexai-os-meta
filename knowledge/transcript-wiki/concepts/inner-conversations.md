# Inner Conversations and Mental Diet

The principle articulated by [[entities/neville-goddard|Neville Goddard]] that the habitual internal dialogue running throughout waking life is the continuous creator of physical circumstances.

## Key Insights

- **No "Casual Imagination"**: Every thought, mental argument, and imagined dispute is an active subconscious impression. Goddard warns that an individual cannot imagine carelessly during the day and expect a brief bedtime visualization to succeed.
- **The Self-Defeating Loop**: Practitioners often assume wealth or health at night, but spend daytime hours internally arguing with colleagues, complaining about bills, or mentally preparing for conflict, thereby neutralizing their assumed state.
- **Mental Diet Discipline**: Consciously arresting discordant internal dialogues and replacing them with conversations spoken from the premise of the fulfilled desire.
