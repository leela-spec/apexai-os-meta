# Sell-the-News Dynamics

An asymmetric price reaction pattern in high-multiple tech stocks during late-stage market cycles.

## Core Mechanisms & Examples

- **Fabrinet Asymmetry**: Beat EPS ($4.10 vs $3.78 estimated), delivered 45% revenue growth (Data Center up ~70%), and raised Q1 outlook above expectations. However, shares plunged -12% due to a minor $13M Datacom miss ($258M vs estimate) on a $1.3B revenue base.
- **Baidu Breakdown**: AI strength was overshadowed by a 23% plunge in legacy revenue and EPS miss (7.22 RMB vs 9.75 RMB expected), leading to a -10% sell-off.
- **Klarna Collapse**: Operating income surged >200% to $91M (double consensus) and revenue rose 27% to $1.04B, but stock plunged -17% on lower transaction volume/profit guidance and German consumer caution.
- **Global Tech Contagion**: European (ASML, Infineon, STM) and Asian (SK Hynix -2%, Samsung -4%, Lenovo) semiconductor pullbacks reflecting crowded trade unwinding.
