# Morning Revision Protocol

A daily mental discipline taught by [[entities/neville-goddard|Neville Goddard]] requiring a dedicated 20-minute session every morning to systematically rewrite memory and alter the trajectory of circumstances.

## Exact Protocol Specifications

1. **Timing & Consistency**: Dedicate exactly **20 minutes** each morning, ideally between 6:00 AM and 8:00 AM, guarded strictly against distraction.
2. **Reviewing Discord**: Review the events, conversations, and disappointments of the preceding day.
3. **Imaginative Rewriting**: Replay each negative scene in imagination, rewriting what was said and done into the ideal, harmonious outcome.
4. **Emotional Naturalization**: Re-experience the revised scene repeatedly until the emotional feeling of the ideal outcome feels more natural and familiar than the memory of the original sensory fact.
5. **Timeline of Manifestation**: Goddard notes that consistent daily practice typically begins visibly altering external circumstances and interpersonal dynamics within **three weeks**.
