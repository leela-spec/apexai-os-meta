# Treasury Yield Pressure

The systemic compression effect exerted on risk assets when benchmark government debt yields rise.

## Yield Levels & Sector Impacts

- **Yield Benchmarks**: 10-year US Treasury yields advancing to 4.74%; 30-year Treasury yields reaching 5.33% (highest level in 19 years).
- **Multiple Compression**: High long-term discount rates disproportionately pressure high-multiple growth equities, causing pre-market NASDAQ declines (-300 points) and exacerbating tech earnings volatility.
- **Geopolitical Backdrop**: Concurrent escalation in Middle East geopolitical tension compounds inflationary pressure on yields.
