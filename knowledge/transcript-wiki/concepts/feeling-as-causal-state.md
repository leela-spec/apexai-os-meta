# Feeling as Causal State

The core epistemological premise in [[entities/neville-goddard|Neville Goddard]]'s philosophy (popularized in his work *Feeling Is the Secret*), asserting that internal subjective feeling is causal rather than reactive.

## Epistemic Mechanics

1. **Causal Inversion**:
   Conventional perception assumes external events produce internal feelings (e.g. having money causes the feeling of wealth). Goddard inverts this: the feeling of wealth experienced in consciousness is the causal origin that externalizes physical money.

2. **Feeling of Need vs. Feeling of Fulfillment**:
   Desiring, hoping, or yearning is recognized as an affirmation of lack, which perpetuates absence. Effective application requires dwelling in the relief and naturalness of the state already achieved.

3. **Subconscious Imprinting**:
   The subconscious mind responds not to words or intellectual concepts, but to felt emotional conviction and sensory acceptance.
