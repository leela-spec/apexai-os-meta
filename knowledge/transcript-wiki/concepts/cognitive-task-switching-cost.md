# Cognitive Task Switching Cost

The empirical latency, error rate elevation, and neural inertia resulting from shifting mental modes.

## Dynamics & Practical Interventions

- **Neural Inertia**: The brain does not operate as an instantaneous step function; residual activity from previous cognitive or emotional tasks bleeds into subsequent tasks.
- **Caltech 5-Minute Silence Protocol**: Implemented by [[entities/ralph-adolphs|Ralph Adolphs]] in Caltech lab meetings (originally instituted in 2020 following George Floyd's death) to quiet mental noise, eliminate residual smartphone/email distractions, and prime cognitive receptivity.
- **Autonomic Conditioning**: Demonstrates that physical practices (such as cold exposure / ice baths) condition autonomic down-regulation, reducing emotional reactivity to extraneous cognitive stressors.
