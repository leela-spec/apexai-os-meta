# Temporal Persistence in Amnesia

Neuropsychological evidence proving that emotional state duration is decoupled from cognitive episodic memory.

## Experimental Paradigm (University of Iowa)

- **Subjects**: Patients with severe bilateral hippocampal damage / dense anterograde amnesia (declarative memory span < 1 minute) vs. healthy controls.
- **Protocol**: Subjects viewed sad film clips, immediately inducing profound sadness in both groups.
- **5-Minute Delay Finding**: After 5 minutes, control participants remembered the movie and reported lingering sadness. Amnesic patients retained zero episodic memory of having watched any film, yet reported feeling deeply sad without knowing why.
- **Significance**: Confirms that emotional states maintain independent temporal persistence time-constants, validating persistence as a core operational feature of emotion.
