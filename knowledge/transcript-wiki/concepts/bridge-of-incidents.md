# Bridge of Incidents

The term used by [[entities/neville-goddard|Neville Goddard]] to describe the series of natural, seemingly coincidental physical occurrences that materialize to bring an assumed state into physical reality.

## Dynamics & Characteristics

- **Autonomous Construction**: Once a state is genuinely assumed in consciousness, the sequence of necessary events (meetings, opportunities, unexpected communications) organizes itself automatically without conscious planning.
- **Naturalness of Occurrence**: When events on the bridge occur, they appear entirely ordinary, logical, or accidental in hindsight (e.g., an unsolicited loan offer, a competitor retiring, an unexpected gift).
- **Warning Against Forcing Means**: Goddard strictly advises practitioners against attempting to force or micro-manage the means of fulfillment, as conscious manipulation stems from doubt and interferes with the assumption.
